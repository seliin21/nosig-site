Delete your NOSIG data

For the app NOSIG: METAR TAF Route Weather on Google Play and the App Store. There is no account in NOSIG, so there is no account to delete. This page explains each kind of data and how to have it deleted.

Ask us to delete your data

  1. Send an email to support@nosigapp.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. If you bought NOSIG Pro, add the order number from your purchase receipt: Google Play (it starts with “GPA.”) or Apple. It is the only way to find your subscription record, because NOSIG does not know who you are.
  3. We delete your subscription record and your emails to us within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting the record does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple Account settings. If the subscription is still active, the app creates a new record the next time it checks the subscription.

What is deleted

  • NOSIG Pro subscription record at RevenueCat, our subscription service: a random app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, the store receipt, your country and the app and system version. Deleted completely; nothing is kept afterwards.
  • Emails you sent us, including feedback: deleted completely once your request is handled.

What is not deleted by us

  • Your purchase and payment history at Google or Apple. We never see it and cannot delete it; Google’s and Apple’s own privacy policies and retention periods apply.

Data on your phone

Favourite airports, airport notes, saved routes, today’s flights, settings and downloaded weather are kept only on your phone and are never sent to us. Delete a note from its airport card. To delete everything at once, uninstall NOSIG, or on Android go to Settings → Apps → NOSIG → Storage → Clear storage.

If you never bought NOSIG Pro, the subscription record holds no purchase and cannot be linked to you.
