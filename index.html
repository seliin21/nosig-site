Every enroute alternate, with the weather for when you pass it.

Enter departure, destination and ETD. NOSIG draws the great-circle route, finds every airport within 140 NM of it and shows each one’s METAR and the TAF for the time you are abeam. Load it before you push back and it still works in airplane mode.

One card for each airport on the way

Tap any airport for the raw METAR, the TAF cut to the moment you pass it with its worst-case category, the best runway for the current wind with head and crosswind, the longest runway and your own notes.

METAR LTFM 101020Z 22008KT 200V260 CAVOK 26/09 Q1016 NOSIG NOSIG: no significant change expected. The word every pilot hopes to read at the end of a METAR.

Departure and destination use your ETD and ETA. Enroute airports use the time you are abeam, worked out from the distance along the route.

Drawn like a chart, not a road map

  • Aviation chart map

    FIR boundaries and names, coastlines and cities, and every airport coloured VFR, MVFR, IFR or LIFR from its latest METAR. Light and dark.

  • SIGMETs on the route

    Thunderstorm, turbulence, icing, mountain wave, volcanic ash and tropical cyclone SIGMETs with ICAO symbols, filtered to your levels, with the times your route enters and leaves each area.

  • SIGWX charts

    WAFS high and mid level significant weather charts, opened at the valid time closest to your flight and kept on the phone. Pinch to zoom.

  • Search and notes

    Find any airport by ICAO, IATA, name or city, and keep your own notes on stands, taxi and fuel for the next time you go there.

  • Your units

    Metres or feet, °C or °F, hPa or inHg. The raw METAR and TAF text is never rewritten.

  • Your corridor

    Choose 100, 140 or 180 NM either side of the route and the shortest runway an alternate needs. Only airports with scheduled service are listed.

Made for airplane mode

Open your route before you push back. In the air NOSIG shows the last weather, SIGMETs and SIGWX charts it downloaded and says exactly how old they are.

Offline · weather from 1056Z (3 h 22 min old)

SIGMETs that expire on the way stay on the map, faded and marked with their end time, until newer data can be loaded.

Where the data comes from

  • METAR, TAF, SIGMETs and SIGWX charts: NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov); SIGWX issued by WAFC Washington
  • Airports and runways: OurAirports (public domain)
  • Coastlines, borders and cities: Natural Earth (public domain)
  • FIR boundaries: VATSpy Data Project, CC BY-SA 4.0

NOSIG is for situational awareness. It is not an official briefing and not for navigation; always use your operator’s briefing and the latest official information.
